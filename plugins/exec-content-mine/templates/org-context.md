# Organization context — instance config (template)

*Copy this into your instance directory as `org-context.md` and fill it in (the `exec-content-mine` skill will interview you at setup if it's missing). This is what makes the skill's output *yours* — it shapes strategy, positioning, tone, audiences, competitive framing, and proof points. Be specific; vague context yields generic ideas.*

## Mission & positioning
*What the organization does, the core bet/thesis, and how it wants to be seen in its market.*

## Products & technology
*The products/pillars, what each is for, key differentiators. Enough that the skill can name them accurately.*

## Target audiences
*Who the content is for — roles, seniority, what they care about, what would make them stop and read.*

## Tone & voice
*How the organization speaks. Bold vs. measured, technical depth, what to avoid.*

## Competitive framing
*Who/what you're implicitly arguing against; the conventional wisdom you complicate.*

## Proof points
*Real, defensible experience — production deployments, scale, outcomes — that competitors can't easily claim. The "privileged knowledge" lens draws on this.*

## Current strategic context
*Anything time-sensitive: recent announcements, market moments, strategic events the content should reflect.*

---
*Optional: point the skill at product/technical reference docs for fact-checking by placing them under `./reference/` (or your configured location). Read on demand, never shipped with the plugin.*
